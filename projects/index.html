Research and software

Models and software for studying changing systems, learning from incomplete data, and comparing interventions and research designs.

My research asks what we can learn from incomplete observations, how to evaluate scientific predictions, and which measurements or interventions to compare. The projects below include released tools and ongoing methods research. The software packages can be used independently or in combination.
