My current analysis portfolio connects mechanistic modeling, Bayesian inference,
and decision-focused evaluation across several private working repositories:

- **Dengue antibody-dependent enhancement:** mechanistic transmission and
  Bayesian analysis of severity and secondary-infection effects
- **Structural scores:** outcome-independent diagnostics for whether scientific
  predictions preserve known dynamical structure
- **COVID-19 observation noise:** controlled experiments on how surveillance
  error propagates into inference and model assessment
- **Diphtheria outbreak vaccination:** operational modeling of outbreak
  response and vaccination strategies
- **CCHF surveillance and control:** cross-scale wildlife and livestock models
  for surveillance-design decisions

The repositories remain private while the analyses and manuscripts mature.
Public artifacts will be linked here as they are released.
