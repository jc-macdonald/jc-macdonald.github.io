I am a computational scientist and {{ current_appointment.position }} at {{
current_appointment.company }}. My overarching interest is how ecological and
landscape change influences the movement and connectivity of hosts, pathogens
and cultural traits, and through them the persistence and evolution of
structured populations. My established work addresses within- and between-host
dynamics and cultural transmission, and landscape-scale questions are a
developing direction. I build mechanistic models, inference methods and
research software for these questions and for decisions under uncertainty. This
page is a concise guide to areas in which I can review proposals or contribute
to a scientific collaboration; my [full CV](/cv/) provides the complete record.

## Review areas

### Established areas

- **Viral and immunological modeling**, including within-host viral growth,
  immune response and antibody kinetics, and links to transmission between
  hosts.
- **Mechanistic modeling of infectious disease dynamics**, including
  within-host, population, spatial, stochastic and multi-scale models, with
  published applications in infectious disease and ecology.
- **Bayesian and simulation-based inference**, including calibration,
  incomplete data, uncertainty assessment and data assimilation.
- **Model, forecast and decision evaluation**, including operational scenario
  modeling, numerical qualification, validation and comparisons under stated
  scientific, cost and resource constraints.

### Developing and collaborative areas

- **Landscape and metapopulation eco-evolutionary models**, asking how
  landscape change, movement and connectivity shape persistence and divergence
  across populations.
- **Scientific machine learning for dynamical systems**, focused on methods
  that incorporate or evaluate scientific structure.
- **Decision-focused observation, monitoring and experimental design**,
  developed jointly with domain and field teams that define the scientific
  decision, feasible measurements and costs.

“Developing” identifies an active methods direction, not a claim of the same
depth of evidence as the established areas.

## Scope

My role is mathematical, statistical and computational. Collaborations on
monitoring or instrument settings require domain partners to define feasible
protocols, calibration procedures and field constraints. I do not present
clinical trial conduct, wet-laboratory virology, wildlife field sampling or
oceanographic instrument engineering as independent areas of expertise.

For a review request or an initial collaboration discussion, please include the
scientific area, decision or review criterion, expected time commitment and any
conflict-of-interest constraints. [Email me](mailto:{{ site.data.socials.email }})
or consult my [publications](/publications/) and [research software](/projects/).
